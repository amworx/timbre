Timbre — interactive prototypes

All five directions are now living apps sharing one simulated player: play, pause, skip, seek, favorite, tune, swipe, open queues — everything responds. Feel each one, then pick below (or describe a mix).

Designer A
Broadcast
Your music as a station you tune

The app is a radio receiver. Home is a program guide of your listening day; you tune between stations (Archive / Flow Queue / Favorites) with a working dial; Now Playing is the faceplate — giant frequency clock, glowing speaker grille, VU-meter progress.

Try: tune the dial · ⌕ opens the Scanner (search) · ARCHIVE lists every track · P1–P3 memory presets = your playlists · ⚙ service menu: day/night faceplate, tape speed, sleep timer · tap the tuner bar for the receiver · VU segments seek · ☰ queue.

Designer B
Museum
Your library as a gallery of works

Every album is an exhibit. Poster feed with caption plates, serif wall-label typography, hairline rules, generous paper whitespace. A single segmented pill navigates (Music / People / Words) — no bottom bar. Now Playing hangs the work in a frame with a museum label.

Try: switch the pill between Music / People / Words · tap a poster to hang it in the frame · tap the label progress hairline to seek · ☀ toggle (top-right of player) for the evening gallery.

Designer C
Tape Deck
Your music on shelves, played on a deck

Tactile hardware, modernized. Home shelves hold cassette spines (playlists & Flow) to flick sideways and record sleeves below. A physical transport bar lives at the bottom of every screen. Now Playing is the deck: cassette window, VU-meter progress, label pocket, A/B keys.

Try: tap a cassette spine (FLOW QUEUE builds a fresh mix) · tap a record sleeve · on the deck, click VU segments to seek · flip SHUF/RPT keys · ☀ key for night deck.

Designer D
Night Journal
A quiet diary of everything you've heard

Built around memory. A journal timeline with big quiet timestamps, three swipe planes (Listen / Browse / Now), dots instead of tabs. Now Playing is typography-first: an ultra-light hero, a slim vertical progress line at the screen edge, controls in the corners.

Try: drag the phone horizontally (or click the dots) to move between planes · tap any journal entry or browse row to play · click the vertical line to seek · ☾-dot toggle in the top corner for the paper edition.

Designer E
Duotone Planes
Fullscreen color, zero chrome, gesture-first

The app is the artwork: everything floats over a duotone gradient tinted by what's playing, chrome reduced to one floating glass dock. Home is a full-bleed mosaic; Now Playing centers the artwork inside a radial progress ring you can tap to seek. Queues and library slide over as planes.

Try: tap mosaic tiles · open ☰ (library) or ≡ (queue) planes from the dock · tap the mini player for Now Playing · tap around the ring to seek · watch the room re-tint with each track.
